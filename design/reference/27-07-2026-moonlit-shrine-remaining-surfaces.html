Remaining surfaces

The eight areas that profile 1.0 and 1.1 left open, now designed. All interface copy is English in plain language: short sentences, no jargon, no exclamation marks. Everything here is live, so hover and press it.

01

Filter bar

The app already has search, genre, format, year range, score range, unrated toggle, sort and select mode. In the old build they sat in one long row that wrapped badly. Here they are split into two lines: text search on top, everything that narrows the list below. Active filters appear as removable chips on a third line, so you can always see why a list looks short.

Two ways in: the checkmark button in the filter bar, or hold any card for half a second. The bar appears above the grid, not floating over it, so nothing is covered. Cards show a circle in the top right corner while select mode is on.

Help

Where is my data saved?

On this computer, in a folder outside the app: %APPDATA%\anime-tracker on Windows. You can delete the app folder and your library stays.

How do I make a backup?

Press the backup button in the header, then Export my library. You get one file with everything. The app also saves a backup on every change and keeps the last 150.

How do I add a series?

Press Add series and search. You can also paste a screenshot of a list, or import your list from MyAnimeList.

A series I watch has a new episode, but the app does not show it.

The schedule comes from AniList. If the series has no schedule there, the app cannot know. Open the series and mark the episode by hand.

Can I use the app without internet?

Yes. Your library, stats, schedule and backups all work offline. Only searching for new series and Discover need a connection.

I matched the wrong series. How do I fix it?

Open the series, then press Replace match and search again. Your episodes and score move to the new match.

What happens when I drop a series?

It moves to Dropped. Watched episodes, your score and your notes are kept, and it stops showing up in Watching and Schedule.

How do I change how the app looks?

Press the settings button. You can pick from 45 themes, change text size and weight, and turn decoration down or off.

How do I update the app?

Download the new version and replace the old folder or the exe. Your data is in a different place, so it is not touched.

Something looks broken. What now?

Reload the page first. If it stays broken, your last backup is in the data folder under backups. Restore it from the backup menu.

Safety screens

The app has two screens that replace everything else: one for an unreadable library file, one for a state with no safe fix, such as two data folders or a library written by a newer version. They must be calm, plain and readable in every theme. No decoration, no atmosphere, no accent glow.

Needs a decision from you

Two data folders found

There is a library in both the old and the new location. The app will not guess which one is right, and it will not touch either.

New location %APPDATA%\anime-tracker\library.json 212 series · changed 6 minutes ago
Old location …\anime-tracker\data\library.json 198 series · changed 3 weeks ago

Move or rename the folder you do not want, then start the app again.

Rule for these two screensNo accent fills, no glow, no decoration, and never a button that guesses. The most helpful thing they can do is state the facts and name the file paths. Text size and theme still apply, since someone who set Largest needs it most when something is wrong.
