Anime Tracker 3.0 is a big update: Discover is rebuilt, the Library is clearer, Home shows what to watch now, and your data is better protected. Your library upgrades by itself: the first start keeps a verified copy of it, then upgrades it. Nothing is removed.

## Highlights

- **Discover, rebuilt.** Recommendations from your own ratings and from AniList's "fans of this also liked", with the reason on every card. Answer with Want to watch, Seen it or Not for me, each with Undo.
- **Swipe through** (press T): one card at a time. Drag it, or use the arrow keys: → Want to watch, ← Not for me, ↑ Seen it, ↓ Skip.
- **A new Library**: tabs for Watching, New episodes, Watchlist, Completed, On hold, Dropped and All, one Filters button, clearer cards and a list view.
- **Home**: continue watching, what airs tonight, what is next on your Watchlist, and this year in numbers.
- **Search and commands** (Ctrl+K): find a series, mark its next episode, jump anywhere.
- **More of your history**: On hold, rewatches, watch dates and a diary, and imports from MyAnimeList, AniList and backup files, each with an Undo.
- **Safer data**: a verified snapshot before every upgrade or import, backups by day and month, and no website can talk to the app.
- **Faster**: a large library opens in about a quarter of the time, Discover in about 0.2 s.
- **A livelier look if you want it**: twelve themes, posters everywhere, and a decoration level from Off to Insane with seasonal particles.

Watched is now called **Completed** and Paused is **On hold**. Nothing moves; only the names change. Press **?** in the app for every keyboard shortcut. The full list of changes is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/czar1337/anime-tracker/blob/v3.0.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Install

1. Download **AnimeTracker.exe** below.
2. Double-click it. Your browser opens the app, and an icon appears in the system tray.

To update from an earlier version, quit the old app from its tray icon and run the new exe. Your data stays where it is, in `%APPDATA%\anime-tracker`.

**Windows SmartScreen:** the exe is not code-signed, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
